Mathematical model

Each city's temperature is an average level, plus two repeating cycles, plus random noise. A Gaussian envelope strengthens the 14-day cycle around day 128 to create a heatwave peak. Time t is in days.

T(t) = T₀ + A₁·E(t)·sin(2πf₁t) + A₂·sin(2πf₂t) + N(t)

Live thermal map

A heat layer over real map coordinates. Each reading is tagged with latitude and longitude, weighted by temperature, and drawn blue for low, yellow for medium and red for high. The map redraws whenever new readings arrive. Zoom with the wheel or the buttons, and drag to pan.
